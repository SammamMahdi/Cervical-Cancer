## Field-level gaps

Synthesized across the corpus through three lenses (clinical translation · ML methodology · data & generalization). The throughline: **near-saturated internal accuracy (98–99.99%) masking weak clinical readiness.**

1. **External / cross-site validation as the default, not the exception** — The dominant evaluation paradigm is train-and-test on the same small public single-cell benchmarks (SIPaKMeD, Herlev, Mendeley LBC) with no held-out site, scanner, or external cohort. Internal-only metrics (98-99.99%) are reported as if they were deployable performance, so methods cannot be ranked against each other…
2. **Domain / scanner / stain / population shift robustness** — A documented, severe (10-30 point) performance collapse under acquisition, staining, scanner, and site variation that the vast majority of papers never measure, let alone mitigate. Domain adaptation, test-time adaptation, stain normalization, and regularization against the external gap are rarely employed or reported.
3. **Dataset monoculture vs. whole-slide clinical reality** — Small, lab-curated, pre-segmented, balanced single-cell datasets stand in for whole-slide reality. The actual clinical bottlenecks (segmentation/detection of overlapping cells in dense regions, debris, inflammation, slide-level triage) remain unsolved, and rare aggressive subtypes are absent.
4. **Slide-level spatial context and rare-event detection (MIL done properly)** — Cancer diagnosis depends on slide-level spatial context and rare-event detection, yet transformer/MIL aggregators are adopted at cell/patch level without modeling spatial arrangement, and strong modern MIL baselines are routinely omitted from comparisons.
5. **Calibration, decision-readiness, and clinical operating points** — Probability calibration is essentially absent (only one paper reports it) despite the screening/triage use case, which depends on trustworthy thresholds (ASC-US+/HSIL+). Uncalibrated, over-confident outputs make threshold selection and clinician trust break down at deployment, and make headline AUCs unverifiable as…
6. **Data leakage, benchmarking rigor, and reproducibility** — Over-optimism from oversampling/augmentation applied before splitting (implausible ~99.99% scores, especially on tabular risk-factor data); no statistical comparison (CIs, significance, multiple seeds); no shared task/protocol; and missing reproducibility infrastructure (no public code, undisclosed splits/sizes,…
7. **Validated efficiency for edge / low-resource deployment** — Knowledge distillation and lightweight design are claimed but under-validated: FLOPs, parameter counts, memory, latency on target field hardware, and student-vs-teacher fidelity are largely unmeasured. The highest-impact setting (low-resource regions) is targeted by few papers, and those underperform.
8. **Federated / privacy-preserving training under realistic heterogeneity** — Federated learning is the main credible path to large, diverse, privacy-compliant cervical datasets, but current work is mostly simulated on the same small public sets, collapses under non-IID conditions, and rarely combines privacy guarantees with client-dropout/heterogeneity testing. 9. **Annotation quality, ground-truth provenance, and label noise** — Annotation quality, inter-observer variability, and ground-truth provenance are largely unaddressed; reliance on cytology-only, single-reader, or synthetic labels bakes in observer bias and label noise that model capacity cannot fix, and makes reported accuracies uninterpretable as agreement with clinical truth.
10. **Trustworthy synthetic data and self-supervised pretraining** — GAN/synthetic augmentation and self-supervised pretraining are used to inflate small datasets without validating that synthetic samples preserve diagnostic morphology or avoid distribution leakage; low-fidelity samples teach artifacts, over-faithful ones cause leakage, and color/stain shortcuts get learned.
11. **Robust multimodal fusion under missing/mismatched modalities** — Multimodal fusion is brittle and unvalidated across sites and depends on complete, matched, co-registered modalities rarely available in practice; gains evaporate when a modality is missing or mis-registered, and near-perfect single-source AUC (~0.999) signals joint-distribution overfitting.
12. **Prospective clinical utility, workflow integration, and regulatory readiness** — Prospective evaluation, reader/workflow-impact studies, end-to-end WSI ingestion, LIS/PACS interoperability, scanner-agnostic pipelines, turnaround-time reporting, and engagement with regulatory/QMS evidentiary standards (FDA/CE/IVDR) are almost entirely absent. ## Top under-explored opportunities (ranked)

1. Create and release a shared, multi-institution, multi-scanner WSI-level cervical benchmark with fixed splits/seeds, a standardized class taxonomy, histopathology-confirmed multi-reader labels, and a leakage-audited public leaderboard. This single artifact unblocks external validation, fair head-to-head benchmarking, and procurement-grade…
2. Make leave-one-site-out external validation plus the internal-vs-external delta the mandatory headline result, reported with calibration (reliability/ECE), pre-specified clinical operating thresholds (ASC-US+/HSIL+), CIs, and significance across multiple seeds, replacing saturated single-number internal accuracy.
3. Reframe the core task from clean single-cell classification to slide-level triage and abnormal-region surfacing with spatial context, benchmarked against proper modern MIL baselines (CLAM/TransMIL/DSMIL) and evaluated on rare-positive sensitivity at fixed low false-positive rates.
4. Deliver validated, deployment-grade efficiency for low-resource/edge settings: report accuracy-retained-vs-teacher alongside FLOPs/params/memory/latency on accessible field hardware under realistic throughput and offline operation, turning unsubstantiated 'lightweight' claims into a measured accuracy-cost frontier.
5. Run real (or rigorously non-IID) multi-site federated studies that jointly quantify the privacy (DP epsilon)-utility-robustness (client dropout/heterogeneity) frontier on external cohorts, moving federation from simulated-on-toy-data to a credible path for large diverse private datasets.
6. Establish robustness-to-shift as a first-class, reported objective: benchmark stain normalization, domain generalization, and test-time adaptation under controlled scanner/stain perturbations, with morphology-vs-color-shortcut probes and accuracy-vs-shift robustness curves.
7. Audit and stamp out data leakage (resample strictly inside CV folds; validate synthetic-data fidelity and enforce synthetic/test separation) and adopt a community reproducibility checklist mandating public code, seeds, splits, and dataset provenance.
8. Conduct prospective, reader-in-the-loop clinical-utility and workflow-impact studies (assisted-vs-unassisted sensitivity, throughput, turnaround time) within an end-to-end LIS/PACS-interoperable, regulatory/QMS-framed pipeline that reports mandatory demographic subgroup performance for equity.

## Relevance to this project's pipeline

Several of these field gaps are shared by the project's own EfficientNet B4→B0 pipeline (see `PIPELINE_DOCUMENTATION.md`): **no external / cross-dataset validation, no probability calibration, the validation set used as the test set, and unvalidated edge-efficiency (FLOPs / latency / params) for the distilled student.** Closing these is the clearest route to a defensible novelty claim.
